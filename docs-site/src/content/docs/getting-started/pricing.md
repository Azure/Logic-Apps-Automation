## Overview

Azure Logic Apps Automation is in public preview. Consult [Azure Logic Apps pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/logic-apps/) for published pricing information, and confirm the applicable preview terms, rates, and any grants with your Azure representative before estimating costs.

## Core platform pricing

Review the Automation environment, workflow runtime, and data retention entries on the pricing page. Confirm which meters and rates apply to your environment.

## Optional capability pricing

Review the pricing page for connector, AI, knowledge, and sandbox usage, and confirm which charges apply to your workflow.

## Free grants

Some environments can show free-grant usage in the portal. If a **View your free grant usage** link appears on your environment overview, select it to open the **Free grants** tab in **Settings**. The tab shows available meters, usage, remaining balances, limits, and reset information. If balances fail to load, use **Retry**.

The tab depends on the environment and available portal features; its absence doesn't by itself establish whether an environment is eligible. Confirm eligibility, current allowances, reset terms, and charges beyond any grant with your Azure representative and the published pricing information.

## Example monthly cost

A worked example isn't currently available. To prepare an estimate, identify your region and expected workflow, connector, AI, and sandbox usage, then confirm the applicable rates and assumptions.
