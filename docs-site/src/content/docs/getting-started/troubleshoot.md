The following tables describe common problems you might experience and their resolutions:

| Problem | Resolution |
|---------|------------|
| "You don't have access to any environment" | Ask your environment administrator to confirm your environment assignment, or create an environment if your organization allows self-service creation. |
| Sign in loop or single sign-on (SSO) error | Contact your administrator for help with your account and sign-in configuration. |
| Portal appears empty | Perform a hard refresh (Keyboard: `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R`). If the problem persists, [report a bug](/support/report-a-bug/). |

| Problem | Resolution |
|---------|------------|
| "I can't create an app." | Ask your environment administrator to confirm the required access. |
| "I can't view any apps in the environment." | Ask your app or environment administrator to check your access. |
| "I can't manage permissions on apps." | Ask your app administrator to confirm the required access. |
| "An app lost its owner." | Contact your environment administrator to confirm the recovery or deletion process. |
| "I can't delete apps." | Confirm the deletion process with your environment administrator. For details, see [Permissions](/features/permissions/). |
